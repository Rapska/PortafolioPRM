About Me

I'm Pietro, and if I had to sum up how I work in one sentence it would be this: I don't stop at diagnosis, I build the solution. I've spent years in international trade, but the habit of looking at a process and asking why it works the way it does — and whether it could work better — goes back long before my first job in the industry.

My first experience leading operations was at MC Cormick Chile, a family business dedicated to building maintenance, between 2014 and 2017. There, with no formal title and no manual to follow, I had to solve real problems with teams of up to 12 people: from redesigning how branch entry documentation was managed nationwide, to building a tool-inventory control system that cut losses. I didn't think of it as "process improvement" at the time — to me it was just solving whatever was in front of me. Years later, already working in international trade, I understood that the approach I'd been following all along had a name: "continuous improvement."

I apply that same logic today at DHL Global Forwarding, where, beyond my role as coordinator, I automate workflows, design KPIs, and lead the standardization of various processes. And also on my own, building RAPSKA, my own digital ecosystem that guides Chileans, Argentinians, and Uruguayans through their migration process to Germany via the Working Holiday visa. I'm currently formalizing that practical approach — studying Lean Six Sigma and Project Management with Google — and learning more and more about artificial intelligence and user-centered design, two areas I use both at work and on RAPSKA.

Outside of work, I've always been drawn to strategy games — from Age of Empires and Team Fight Tactics to board games and trading card games like Catan, Magic: The Gathering, or Pokémon TCG — probably because it's the same mindset I use to design processes, just applied to a different board. I also practice martial arts (karate when I was younger, taekwondo more recently) and enjoy cycling. Lately I've been spending time learning about AI and UX/UI out of personal curiosity, beyond anything formal, and there was a stretch when I learned a lot about video editing and recording, mainly when my YouTube channel was more active.
