User experience & digital product · Case 06

RAPSKA — turning a complex migration process into a guided experience

My own brand and digital product helping Spanish speakers through the Germany Working Holiday — from zero to an app with an active community.

RAPSKA · Own project Visit rapska.com
Role

Founder — product design, UX, brand, and AI-assisted build

Tools

Lovable · Claude · Gemini + Firebase (AI prototyping)

Status

App on Android, site in production

The problem

Applying for the Working Holiday in Germany means navigating dozens of requirements and paperwork — passport, health insurance, proof of funds, the VIDEX form, an embassy appointment, flight ticket, cover letter, resume, housing — scattered across different official sources, with nowhere that organizes the process end to end or explains what order to do things in.

What I built

I designed and built RAPSKA: a brand and ecosystem — website, app, a WhatsApp community of close to 1,000 people, and a weekly newsletter — to guide Spanish speakers through the whole process, from the moment someone first gets the idea to when they're already settled and building a life in Germany. I organized that journey into several stages (Decision → Preparation → Application → Travel → Arrival → First Steps) and designed "My Route": a milestone system with real dependencies between steps — you can't book the embassy appointment before meeting the proof-of-funds requirement, and you can't buy the flight ticket before having the appointment.

Product decisions

I considered adding a chatbot to the website and decided against it, after weighing the idea against basic usability principles: one goal per page, a single CTA, clear separation of roles. The site is built to attract search traffic, and a chatbot there would compete with conversion to app downloads. Instead, I designed RapskAI, a conversational assistant with topic limits inside the app — built for someone already in the process who needs step-by-step guidance, not for someone who's just starting to look for information.

AI-assisted prototyping

To build the app I evaluated and tested different AI-assisted prototyping tools — Lovable, Claude, and Gemini + Firebase — before settling on an approach, based on how well each one translated the product's logic into a real interface.

Current status

The app is live on Android, with around 20 active users (as of October 2026), and the site at rapska.com is in production. It's an early-stage product — what I can show with the most confidence right now are the product decisions, not yet metrics at scale.

What I learned

A product decision isn't always consistent when looked at in isolation. Turning down a chatbot in one place and building one in another can look like a contradiction, until you realize each one answers to a different audience and a different goal.
